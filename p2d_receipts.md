## New gotchas (all measured this session)

- **Named-arg `by` blocks elaborate AFTER the application's implicit state is
  assigned; term arguments elaborate BEFORE.**  So `(hi := by simp only
  [evalW_var, w_charge, w_storeW, hy])` sees a concrete goal and closes, while
  `(hi := congrArg some hy)` fails to unify (`evalW ?s …` cannot reduce a meta
  state).  Corollary: never assign a metavariable from a by-block whose
  sibling args still need it — the first closing tactic grabs the assignment
  (measured: `rw [evalW_var]` auto-rfl assigned the UNPEELED term to `j`,
  breaking the `hj` bound).
- **A `by` block with same-line tactics ends at EOL.**  Continuation lines
  must be indented deeper than the enclosing line (not the `by` column), or
  the parser ends the block at the newline ("unexpected identifier; expected
  ')'").  Multi-tactic named-arg blocks: either fully single-line or `by` at
  end-of-line.
- **Anonymous constructor notation `⟨_,_⟩` unfolds defs at REDUCIBLE
  transparency in term position.**  `⟨?_, ?_⟩` against `RelLog …` (a plain
  def) fails to see the conjunction; tactic-position `refine ⟨?_, ?_⟩` DOES
  unfold it.  Term-position: use named-goal `?hR`-style metavariables plus
  bullets instead.
- **`if_pos`/`if_neg` need the EXACT condition syntactically.**  A pre-simp
  that reduces `"rgt" = "rgt"` to `True` (leaving `True ∧ x = stW.lft x`)
  breaks a later `if_neg hx2`.  Handle the split BEFORE peels, or prove
  against the reduced form.
- **rw-lemma ARGUMENT by-blocks (`rw [evalW_load_of (by …) (by …)]`) are
  deferred and get concrete goals after the rewrite unifies; term-position
  arguments of `runs_wstore` do not.**  Don't mix the two mental models.
- **`simp only` does NOT instantiate metavars, `rw` DOES** (with the peeled
  term, which may be the wrong one).  Peel with `simp only`, close with `rw
  [fact]`.
- Hand-counted paren/peel chains were the session's #1 cost.  The state terms
  are programmatically generatable — generate them (see the python `chain()`
  in this session's flow) rather than counting.
